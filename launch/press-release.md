# PickAPlugin press release

## Full version (~400 words)

**Headline:** PickAPlugin Launches a Hand-Sorted, Independent Directory of ChatGPT and Codex Plugins

**Subhead:** The free catalog lists 344 plugins across 12 categories, sorted by people instead of an algorithm, and clearly labels any paid placement.

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

October 2026: PickAPlugin (https://pickaplugin.com) has launched as an independent, hand-sorted catalog of plugins, skills and extensions for ChatGPT and Codex. The site is free to browse. It is meant to help developers and AI users find useful tools without digging through scattered GitHub repositories and social media threads.

The number of community-built plugins for AI coding agents has grown fast, and so has the work of telling them apart. PickAPlugin sorts each project into a plain category: Agent Workflows, Memory & Context, Review & Testing, Security & Governance, Docs/Content & SEO, Design & Frontend, Deploy & DevOps, Usage & Quotas, Messaging & Remote, Data & Backend, Industry Tools, and Miscellany. Each listing has its own page with a short summary written from the project's own documentation, plus GitHub details such as star count, last update, license, language and install command.

The front page is laid out like a newspaper. Editors' picks are labelled as opinion. A public "How we pick" page explains how projects are chosen, and a changelog records what has changed on the site. PickAPlugin is independent and not affiliated with OpenAI.

**For plugin makers**, PickAPlugin offers:

- **Claim your listing:** makers can verify their project and keep its details current.
- **README badges:** a free badge that links back to the project's listing.
- **Labelled placements:** optional paid featured spots and classifieds. These are always marked as paid and never change editorial picks or catalog order.

Readers can also sign up for an occasional email roundup of new and notable plugins.

"Good plugins were getting lost in long threads and unsorted lists," said the PickAPlugin team. "We wanted one place where a person has actually sorted them, and where it's always clear what's an opinion and what's paid for."

Makers can suggest a plugin, claim a listing or ask about placements at https://pickaplugin.com.

**About PickAPlugin**
PickAPlugin is an independent directory of ChatGPT and Codex plugins, sorted by people and free for visitors.
