Note: the poster says these are mostly nofollow and not guaranteed. Signup rules are based on public pages and general knowledge, and each one must be re-checked at signup. Nothing has been signed up for yet.

## Ranked viable
1. **Hugging Face** — https://huggingface.co/join (email + username + password, free). Make a Space or dataset at https://huggingface.co/new-space or /new-dataset: "pickaplugin-catalog", a dataset of the 344 plugins (name, category, repo, license) exported from the catalog. Fields: name, license, README. README copy: "Open dataset of 344 ChatGPT & Codex plugins, hand-sorted into 12 categories by PickAPlugin (https://pickaplugin.com). Updated from GitHub metadata." Highly relevant, with a real dev audience.
2. **Hashnode** — https://hashnode.com/onboard (email magic link or GitHub). Fields: blog name, handle, then post. Post: "344 Codex/ChatGPT plugins, sorted: what we learned" (rework the press-release body plus the categories), with a canonical link to pickaplugin.com/about. Dev audience.
3. **Bear Blog** — https://bearblog.dev/signup/ (email + password, free). Fields: subdomain (pickaplugin.bearblog.dev), title, bio with a link. Post the full press release from press-release.md plus a weekly "new plugins" note.
4. **1888PressRelease** — https://www.1888pressrelease.com/ → "Submit Free PR" (free tier; account needs name/email, and a phone field may be required, so check). Fields: headline, summary, body, category (Computers/Internet), contact. Copy: the headline, subhead and body from press-release.md.
5. **Express Press Release** — https://express-press-release.net/free/ (free form, editorial review that can take weeks; check for a phone field). Same press-release copy.
6. **Flipboard** — https://flipboard.com/signup (email). Make a magazine called "AI Coding Plugins" and add the SubmitPR release, our posts, and good third-party plugin articles. Low SEO value.
7. **CodePen** — https://codepen.io/accounts/signup (email). Profile has up to 3 links: pickaplugin.com, /badge example, X. You could make a pen showing the README badge embed. Medium relevance.
8. **Ko-fi / Beacons** — https://ko-fi.com/account/register, https://beacons.ai/signup (email). Profile link-in-bio only. Low value. Ko-fi would also be a second payment channel, which you shouldn't set up (Stripe-only policy).

## Skip
- **Press Release Submit** (pressreleasesubmit.com): only one free release, and it needs a "verified business" account, which likely means a phone or documents. Paid after that.
- **Manta, Cybo, Alignable**: local/SMB business directories that usually want a physical address and phone. Not relevant to us (Alignable is US/CA local). Manta/Cybo returned 403 to bots.
- **Flickr**: photography only, irrelevant.
- **Observable**: data-viz notebooks. Only worth it if we publish a real chart of the catalog later (it was rate-limited when checked).

## Local business directories (email + address; phone or postcard is the risk)
- Bing Places (https://www.bingplaces.com): verification is by phone, email, or postcard; it can import from Google. A postcard to the Unit 501 address is possible if mail arrives there. Low relevance.
- CitySquares (https://citysquares.com/add_business), Fyple (https://fyple.com), Opendi (https://opendi.us, 403 to bots), Cylex (https://cylex.us.com, 403 to bots): usually email + address, with phone often a required field. Low value. Do them only if a phone isn't forced.
- Chamber of Commerce (https://www.chamberofcommerce.com): the free listing usually wants a phone. Low value.

## Skip (need phone or postcard verification, or aren't relevant)
- Google Business Profile: needs postcard, video, or phone verification, and is meant for businesses with a storefront or service area. An online-only directory isn't eligible for Maps.
- Apple Business: needs a phone call or document verification.
- Yelp: needs phone verification and is a local review site.
- Superpages / DexKnows (Thryv): phone-based claims.
- Nextdoor Business: needs a local neighborhood and phone, so it isn't relevant.
