## SUBMIT

These are the only three with a public free form and no Aaron login step.

### 1. Launching Next

- Site: https://www.launchingnext.com/
- Submit: https://www.launchingnext.com/submit/ (trailing slash required; `/submit` without it 404s)
- Free path: yes. Official form says submitting is free. A $99 upgrade on the next page is optional (publication considered within 1 business day). They review daily and email if published.
- Links: official pages do not state dofollow/nofollow. No badge required.
- Signup: public form, no account.
- Required fields: Startup Name; Startup URL; headline (5–8 words); full description (2,500 characters max); 5–10 comma-separated tags; submission type (side project / bootstrapped startup / investor funded / none of these); planned 90-day marketing spend ($0 through $15,000+); Your Name; Your Email; math check ("What is 2+3?"). Newsletter opt-in is optional. Use rounded "450+ plugins" copy, no fake stats.

### 2. Startup Buffer

- Site: https://startupbuffer.com/
- Submit: https://startupbuffer.com/site/submit
- Free path: yes. Official FAQ: "You can submit a startup for review without paying." Promotion packages are separate and optional. "Creating an account is not mandatory for submission."
- Links: official FAQ does not state dofollow/nofollow. No badge required.
- Signup: public form. Account optional (password reset exists for people who later create one). Cloudflare may challenge some bots; the page is live.
- Required fields: startup name; website URL; business email; short pitch; full description; product/homepage screenshot (PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, or HEIC, max 15MB). Category, location, and tags are optional. They send a private edit link.

### 3. AppRater

- Site: https://apprater.net/
- Submit: https://apprater.net/#submit (also linked as "Submit app")
- Free path: yes. Public homepage form, no payment step. The archive includes Web products, not only downloadable/store apps, so a catalog site fits as a Web app.
- Links: official pages do not state dofollow/nofollow. No badge required.
- Signup: no account required to submit (name + email only).
- Required fields: App name; platform (choose Web); Main app or store URL (`https://pickaplugin.com`); Launch status (Available now); What makes it useful?; Your name; Email. Optional: extra platform URLs, tags, image URL, X username.
